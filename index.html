How does this tool work?

Paste messy phone numbers directly into the input area. The tool automatically extracts numbers, strips out special formatting, and converts national numbers into international standard WhatsApp formats.

  • +62 812-3445-3444
  • 0812(3456)7890
After Formatting (Clean Output):
  • 6289695309913
  • 6281234453444
  • 6281234567890
